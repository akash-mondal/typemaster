# FLOW sprite credits

Character animation in `atlas.png` is derived from:

**Platformer Animations** by **Clint Bellanger**
https://opengameart.org/content/platformer-animations
Licence: **CC-BY 3.0** — attribution required.

The 64x64 mannequin frames were scaled to a 34px standing figure, quantised to
this project's palette, and painted over with costume detail (hood, headband,
face band, sash, wraps, scarf, sword; and a ronin variant with a straw kasa).
The derived sprites remain subject to CC-BY 3.0.

This credit must be shown to players — see the in-game credits screen.
