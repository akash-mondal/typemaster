# NOVA art references

`tools/novaart/build.py` cuts NOVA's ships, effects and pickups from these packs. They are not
in git (29 MB). Download them into `tools/novaart/ref/<folder>/` to rebuild the atlas.
All are CC0: no attribution is required, but the game credits them in its manifest.

Used by the current build: `foozle-void` and `grafxkid-mini-pixel-pack-3`. The others were
downloaded as references only.
